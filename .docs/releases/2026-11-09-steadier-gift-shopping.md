# Shopping for a friend feels steadier - November 9, 2026

Gift options on a friend's wishlist no longer jump around while you use them.

- Reserving or releasing an item updates the reservation panel in place instead
  of flashing a spinner.
- After you reserve, a "View my gift plan" link takes you straight to your plan.
- Comments stay put when the list refreshes, so a half-written comment and your
  cursor are not lost.
- Press Ctrl+Enter (or Cmd+Enter) to send a comment. Comments are trimmed, show
  dates like "Nov 9, 2026 3:05 PM", and return focus to the box after sending.
- The owner's gift hints load once per list instead of reloading every time the
  list changes.

No privacy rules changed: owners still never see reservations.
