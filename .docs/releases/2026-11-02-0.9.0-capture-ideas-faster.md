# Capture ideas faster, with clearer feedback - November 2, 2026

Adding and curating ideas on a wishlist is quicker and more truthful.

- "Add and add another" in the Add item dialog saves an idea, clears the form,
  and puts the cursor back on the product link so you can capture several
  ideas in one visit.
- Confirmations stay visible near the top of the screen after an action far
  down a long list, then clear themselves. Errors stay until dismissed.
- If a search or filter hides the idea you just added, OpenWish says so and
  offers "Show all ideas".
- "Copy link" shares the plain wishlist address (without view-only options
  such as a budget) and the button briefly reads "Link copied".
- Searching ignores spaces typed before or after the words, and pressing
  Escape in the search box clears it.

Nothing about who can see a list or an idea changes.
