# Calmer Friends page - November 20, 2026

Journey J3, "Connect": the Friends page is easier to use on a phone and says
what happens next.

- **Sent requests fit phones.** Long email addresses wrap, the Resend and Cancel
  buttons are full-size tap targets, and rows no longer overflow.
- **Readable loading.** Friends and friend requests show "Loading..." text in a
  reserved space instead of a bare spinner that makes the page jump.
- **Feedback where you look.** "Friend removed" now appears above the friend
  list instead of below a long list.
- **Next step after inviting.** Sending email invitations says to follow them
  under Sent requests.
- **Next step after a suggestion.** Adding a suggested person says the same.

No schema changes.
