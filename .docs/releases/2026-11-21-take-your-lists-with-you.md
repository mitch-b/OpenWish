# Take your lists with you - November 21, 2026

Roadmap E2, "Export your data": your lists are yours to keep, analyze, and move.

- **Wishlist CSV.** "Download CSV" on a list saves the ideas you can see
  (name, price, store, link, priority, most wanted, notes). Reservations,
  purchases, and comments are never included, so an owner's file holds no
  surprises.
- **Gift plan CSV.** "Download CSV" on My gift plan saves every gift with its
  price, event budget, and status (To buy, Bought, Wrapped) for a budget
  spreadsheet. Only you can see this plan.
- **Events CSV.** "Download CSV" on Events saves event dates, budgets, and
  organizers. Emails, participants, and draw results are left out.
- **Export your lists.** Account > Personal data has "Download my wishlists",
  a JSON file of every list you own. Ideas that collaborators hid from you as a
  surprise are not included.
- **Import.** Choose an exported file on the same page to add its lists as new
  lists. They start private, a list that shares a name with one of yours gets
  " (imported)" added instead of replacing it, and only http and https links are
  kept. Files up to 2 MB, 100 lists, and 1,000 ideas per list are accepted.

Spreadsheet cells that begin with `=`, `+`, `-`, or `@` are prefixed with an
apostrophe so a shared list cannot run a formula on your computer.

No schema changes.
