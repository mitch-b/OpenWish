# Connected-app permission choices

Connected apps and AI assistants can now help curate ideas already on your
lists, not just the ideas they added.

- **Manage existing items in allowed lists** is checked for new connections.
  It lets the app see, edit, move, and remove ideas you or other apps added.
  Clear it for more privacy: only ideas that connection added are accessible.
- **Allow all wishlists** is off by default. Check it to include every wishlist
  you own, including private lists and lists you create later.
- Change either choice in Integrations and save permissions. Changes apply to
  the current connection without needing a new token.

Existing connections keep their earlier, narrower access until you explicitly
change it or reconnect. Reservations, purchases, comments, and hidden surprises
remain private in every mode. Existing ideas keep their original source and
privacy settings when an app edits them.
