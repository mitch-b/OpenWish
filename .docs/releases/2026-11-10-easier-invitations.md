# Inviting your group is easier - November 10, 2026

Organizers can now invite and gather their group without leaving the event page.

- A new event opens with the invitation box at the top of the page. The
  "Invite people" setup step moves you straight to it.
- Paste several addresses and a progress bar shows each invitation as it sends.
- If someone never answers, choose "Mark as accepted" to add them yourself.
  People without an account get a name you choose, and they are included when
  you draw names.
- Invitees choose how to join. The invitation email offers "Join with an
  OpenWish account" and "Continue without an account", and the sign-up and
  sign-in pages keep the no-account option in case someone taps the wrong
  button. Organizers no longer pick one way for everyone.
- Invitation and gift-exchange emails now share the app's look and read well on
  phones and computers.
- Shared and friends' wishlists show whose list it is under the title.
- Wishlist and event cards are more compact. Click the card or its title to
  open it; the separate "Open" row is gone.
- "Run it again" no longer appears on a brand-new event; it shows after the
  event is over or names are drawn.

Privacy is unchanged: only the organizer can accept for someone else, and it is
not possible after names are drawn.
