# Wishlist refinement - September 24, 2026

Twenty focused refinements make it easier to capture an idea and coordinate a
gift without losing the wishlist's privacy context:

1. Put the create action ahead of secondary wishlist discovery controls.
2. Explain wishlist discovery in terms of saved and shared gift ideas.
3. Give first-time owners a specific way to start an empty collection.
4. Distinguish no search matches from no wishlists, with a reset action.
5. Remove redundant creator labels from a person's own wishlist cards.
6. Let long wishlist titles and creator names wrap instead of clipping.
7. Make card metadata readable without relying on very small text.
8. Use a calm, unshadowed wishlist heading instead of another gradient hero.
9. Show collaboration and private-list context next to the wishlist title.
10. Place the add-item action with the wishlist's primary actions.
11. Keep action labels visible and the add action full-width on phones.
12. Call the clipboard action "Copy link" so its result is predictable.
13. Make the comments count a real keyboard-accessible button.
14. Keep passive counts visually quiet instead of suggesting they are clickable.
15. Count advanced filters separately from the text search.
16. Keep price controls within the item range, including expensive ideas and
   crossed minimum/maximum values.
17. Treat "reserved" and "available" together as all items, not no items.
18. Explain empty lists differently to contributors and gift shoppers.
19. Replace star-only priority and icon-only product links with readable labels
   in the grid; provide gift coordination from that view.
20. Give mobile item rows visible field labels and specific gift-option actions,
   while making URL import optional, retryable, and consistent across both
   item-entry forms.
