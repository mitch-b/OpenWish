# Shop for your match with less friction - October 31, 2026

Shopping for a gift exchange match is steadier and shows where you stand.

- Your match stays on screen while the event page finishes loading instead of
  flashing back to a spinner.
- The match shows how soon the exchange is: today, tomorrow, or in N days.
- My Reserved Items totals what you reserved and, in a gift exchange, how much
  of the budget is left or exceeded, and how many items have no price.
- Wishlist links in My Reserved Items open in the same tab.
- An empty My Reserved Items links to the event wishlists.

These details are private to the shopper; recipients never see them.
