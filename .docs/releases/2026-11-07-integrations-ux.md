# Smaller, clearer integrations - November 7, 2026

Connect an AI assistant by copying the MCP address at the top of Integrations,
then sign in and choose its lists. Setup help and app developer details stay
out of the way until you need them. Clipboard feedback tells you whether the
address was copied or needs to be selected manually.
Integrations now uses the full page instead of repeating the account header
and settings navigation; existing links and bookmarks still work.

Connections now show a compact summary with their destination, allowed-list
count, usage, and ideas added. Choose **Adjust lists** to open only that app's
editor, change access and the default list, or cancel without saving.

Both connection approval and editing use a searchable, scrollable list picker
with a **Selected only** filter and live selection counts. Hidden selections
remain selected when saving, and the default selector follows allowed lists.
Large collections stay compact: idea counts replace item feeds, and permission
forms load only when requested. Reservations, purchases, comments, friends,
and ideas added outside that connection are still never shared with an app.
