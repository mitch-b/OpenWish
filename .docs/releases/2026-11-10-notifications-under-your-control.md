# Notifications stay under your control - November 10, 2026

The notification panel now does what you tell it to, and nothing more.

- Moving the pointer over a notification, or touching one while scrolling on a
  phone, no longer marks it read. Use the check button on a notification, open
  it with "View", or choose "Mark all as read".
- Times read "5 min ago", "1 day ago", or a short date, so they are right in
  any time zone. Hover a time to see the exact moment in UTC.
- When you accept or decline an event invitation from a notification, it shows
  your answer instead of offering the buttons again.
- "Show more" reveals older notifications instead of stopping at 20.
- On phones the notification buttons are at least 44 px, and unread
  notifications are announced to screen readers.

Privacy is unchanged: you only ever see your own notifications.
