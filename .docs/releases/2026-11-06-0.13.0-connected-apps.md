# Bring gift ideas from your other apps - November 6, 2026

Software you run, such as a wardrobe tracker or reading notebook, can send gift
ideas to OpenWish and keep its own ideas up to date.

- Nothing is connected automatically. The OpenWish operator registers an app,
  then each person reviews its consent screen and chooses its lists.
- Select existing lists or start a new private list. A newly created list is
  where new ideas go, even when existing lists are also selected.
- Ideas show "From {app}", and editing an app-added idea explains that the app
  may update its details again. The edit flow also works after WebAssembly
  activation without adding a duplicate idea.
- Account settings > Connected apps lets you change list access, choose a
  default, or disconnect. Ideas already added stay on your lists.
- Apps never receive reservations, purchases, comments, friends, or ideas they
  did not add. Disconnects and list changes also invalidate unused consent codes.

## Connect an AI assistant

Operators can turn on an MCP server so people can connect Claude, ChatGPT,
GitHub Copilot in VS Code, and other assistants that support remote MCP servers
with sign-in.

- Copy the server address from Account settings > Connected apps and add it to
  your assistant. You approve it on the same consent screen and choose its lists.
- Ask the assistant to save, change, or remove gift ideas. It sees only the
  lists you chose and the ideas it added.
- Because assistants name themselves, the consent screen warns you to connect
  only one you just set up, and shows where it will send you back.
- Assistant access expires within an hour unless the assistant keeps it fresh,
  and disconnecting stops it right away.

Moving an idea away from a shared list now hides its old activity entries from
that list and the friends feed, so later private edits do not leak through them.

Operator setup and the supported server-side client contract are in
[Connected apps](../INTEGRATIONS.md).
