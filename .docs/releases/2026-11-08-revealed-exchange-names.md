# Clearer exchange names and wishlist sharing

After an organizer reveals an exchange, participants with email-style account
usernames now appear by their safe account handle instead of as “Invited guest.”
Names a participant chose for the group remain visible, and account email
addresses stay private.

Event wishlists now use compact participant rows, with status and actions kept
together. Sharing controls stay close to the wishlist they affect, and button
labels remain aligned and unbroken on narrow screens.
