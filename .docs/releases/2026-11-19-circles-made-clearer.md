# Circles, made clearer - November 19, 2026

Follow-ups to friend circles (roadmap C1) so sharing through a circle is easy
to read and never misleading.

- **Who sees it, and why.** On a list's sharing page, friends who see it through
  a circle are labelled "via Book club".
- **Honest removal.** Removing someone who only sees a list through a circle no
  longer says their access was removed. It explains that they still see it
  through the circle and how to end that.
- **Event invitations.** Circle buttons show how many members are still to
  invite ("Family (2 of 4 not invited)") and are disabled once everyone is invited.
- **Circles lead to lists.** A circle's shared lists link to their sharing page,
  and choosing a circle on Friends offers "View lists from <circle>".
- **Empty circles explain themselves.** On Friends' wishlists, a circle with no
  members or no shared lists says so instead of "No shared wishlists match".

No schema changes. Circles remain private to their owner.
