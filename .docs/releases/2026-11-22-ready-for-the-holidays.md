# Ready for the holidays - November 22, 2026

A polish release before the gift-giving season: thirty focused passes across
every core journey, with phones as the first-class screen.

- **Wishlists on phones.** Lists, ideas, and the add-idea form fit a 390px
  screen without sideways scrolling. Actions are full-size touch targets, and
  item details wrap instead of overflowing.
- **Adding ideas.** Quick add, the link importer, and the item form share one
  layout. Blank names and negative prices are rejected by the server as well as
  the form.
- **Gift exchanges.** Organizers see the next step first, resetting a draw
  takes one clear confirmation, and participants confirm before revealing
  matches to everyone. Guests see who organized the event.
- **Reservations and gift plan.** Reservation, comment, and gift-plan states
  read clearly and use consistent busy labels while saving.
- **Friends and circles.** Requests, search, suggestions, and Connect in person
  use the same layout and icons.
- **Account.** Settings pages share one heading scale, save buttons are normal
  width on desktop, the phone menu is a tidy grid, and recovery codes are easier
  to copy. The lockout page says how long to wait (15 minutes).
- **Connected apps.** Consent pages say what an app can do and what it never
  sees.
- **What's new and install.** Paging works before the page finishes loading,
  and installing explains its steps once.

Every page is now checked automatically on phone and desktop, in light and dark
themes, for sideways scrolling, unnamed buttons, small touch targets, and
errors.

No schema changes.
