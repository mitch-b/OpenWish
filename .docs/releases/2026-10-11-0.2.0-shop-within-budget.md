# Shop within your gift exchange budget - October 11, 2026

Shoppers can now use a gift exchange's suggested budget directly while
choosing an idea. OpenWish keeps the recipient's reservations private while
making price decisions faster.

- The gift-match action now opens the recipient's wishlist with the exchange
  budget attached, rather than making a shopper remember it.
- The wishlist says how many priced ideas fit that budget and starts with
  those ideas shown.
- A clear one-tap control lets shoppers include every idea again without
  losing the budget context.
- The existing sort controls now include **Budget fit**, putting ideas within
  the budget before over-budget and unpriced ideas.
- Every idea shows whether it is within budget, how much it exceeds it, or
  that its price is not set. The same status is available in both grid and
  list views.

The budget is carried only in the shopper's browser route. It does not expose
reservations or change what the wishlist owner can see.
