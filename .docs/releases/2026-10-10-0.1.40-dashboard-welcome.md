# A dashboard welcome that keeps up with you - October 10, 2026

The signed-in dashboard used to open with the same large banner on every
visit: "Welcome Back!", "Your gift season, together.", and two buttons that
repeat the shortcuts further down the page. It helps on your first visit. After
that, it took most of a laptop screen and the entire first screen on a phone
before you reached your own plans.

The large welcome is now just for getting started. It appears until you own a
wishlist or belong to an event. If someone invited you to an event before you
created anything, it leads with that invitation and links to it, then offers
to create a wishlist so your group knows what you'd love. Otherwise it offers
to create your first wishlist or start a gift exchange.

After that, a compact header takes its place. It keeps the branded colors at
about a third of the height and says one thing that matters now, with one
action:

- **An invitation:** "You're invited to Holiday Gift Exchange." with the event
  date and a link to review it. Several invitations are summarized.
- **An event in the next 30 days:** "Holiday Gift Exchange is in 21 days."
  Gift exchanges add whether names have been drawn and the budget. Organizers
  are reminded to draw names, and participants to keep their wishlist current.
  The header never names your match, so the dashboard is safe to show on a
  shared screen.
- **A friend request:** who sent it, or how many are waiting, with a link to
  review them.
- **Nothing waiting:** "You're all caught up." with your next event further
  out, or a short summary of your wishlists.

Event countdowns use the event's own calendar date, so an event happening
today still appears as "today", and it now stays in Upcoming events for the
whole day instead of dropping out at midnight. Needs attention labels use
sentence case. While the dashboard loads, a placeholder the same size as the
compact header holds its place, and its animation stops when reduced motion is
requested. Once the welcome appears, it carries over as the page becomes
interactive instead of dropping back to the placeholder.

Unit tests cover the full welcome with and without invitations, each header
message in priority order, singular and plural wording, today, tomorrow, and
30-day countdowns across time zones, drawn and undrawn exchanges for
organizers and participants, missing names, and the wishlist summary. The
browser journey checks the full welcome and invitation link for a new invitee
on desktop and mobile, and that it stays in place while the page becomes
interactive. It also checks the returning owner's countdown header, its event
link and touch target, that it hides the match, that it has a single page
heading, and its height on mobile.
