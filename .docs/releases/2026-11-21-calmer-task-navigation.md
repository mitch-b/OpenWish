# Calmer task navigation - November 21, 2026

Friends, gift exchanges, and settings now put one task at a time in view.

- **Friends.** Move between your friends, requests, invitations, in-person
  connections, and circles using the section menu.
- **Gift exchanges.** Find your match, invitations, wishlists, and organizer
  controls in separate sections. The page opens to the next useful step.
  Run an event again after its scheduled time has passed.
- **Settings.** Event, wishlist, and account settings group related forms and
  give sharing and deletion their own sections.
- **Phones and tablets.** The menu moves above the content and wraps to fit.
  Switching interactive sections keeps an unfinished form in place.

No schema changes.
