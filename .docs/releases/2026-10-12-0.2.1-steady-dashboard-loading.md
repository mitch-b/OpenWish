# A steady dashboard while OpenWish connects - October 12, 2026

Your dashboard now stays in place while its interactive controls connect, so
you can keep reading and act without a distracting loading flash.

- Quick add stays ready for the next idea you want to save.
- The Needs attention panel keeps its invitations, requests, and event status visible.
- Recently updated wishlists remain on screen instead of briefly disappearing.
- Coming up keeps your events and exchanges in place during hydration.
- The dashboard now verifies this behavior in the browser, protecting the complete page from returning to a loading state after it renders.
