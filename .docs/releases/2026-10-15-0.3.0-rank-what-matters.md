# Put your most wanted ideas first - October 15, 2026

Wishlist owners can now arrange gift ideas in the order that best reflects
what they want. Shoppers see that same intentional order, so they can begin
with the ideas that matter most.

- Mark an idea as **Most wanted** to keep it at the top of the wishlist.
- Move ideas up or down from either grid or list view. The controls work with
  a mouse, touch, or keyboard and say exactly which idea they affect.
- The order is saved immediately and remains in place the next time anyone
  opens the wishlist.
- Owner order is now the default view. Existing priority, budget, price, name,
  and date sorts are still available when they are more useful.
- Shoppers see the most-wanted marker and owner-defined order, but not the
  controls that can change them.

Ranking is restricted to the wishlist owner. It does not reveal reservations,
comments, or private ideas to shoppers.
