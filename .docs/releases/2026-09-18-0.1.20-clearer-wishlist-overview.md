# OpenWish 0.1.20: Clearer wishlist overview

## Summary

Your wishlist overview now makes the size and sharing status of your
collection clear before you begin searching. The shared-with-you tab also
explains what belongs there and points to the next useful step when it is
empty.

## Highlights

- A collection pulse shows total wishlists, saved ideas, and shared lists.
- Shared wishlists have a clear heading and a direct route to friend
  management.
- An empty shared-with-you tab explains what is missing and offers a
  purposeful Find friends action.
- Browser verification retries a route once when its expected server-rendered
  content is incomplete, making CI resilient to transient navigation timing.
