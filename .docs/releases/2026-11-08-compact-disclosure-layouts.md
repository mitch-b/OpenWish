# Less scrolling when controls open - November 8, 2026

Wishlist filters now use a compact two-column layout on wider screens instead
of stacking every group down the page. On phones they remain a readable single
column, with less space between labels and controls. Filters still start closed;
opening or closing them does not clear your choices.

Event type and exchange-style choices size themselves around their content
instead of reserving tall empty panels. Choosing a celebration still hides
exchange styles, and switching back keeps the same familiar setup flow.

The add-item dialog has smaller header and body insets while keeping its
scrollable form and reachable actions. The full-page idea form places related
optional fields side by side on wider screens. Labels, help, privacy choices,
validation, and touch-sized controls remain available.
