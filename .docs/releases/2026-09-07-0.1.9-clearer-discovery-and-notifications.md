# OpenWish 0.1.9

Wishlist discovery, friend invitations, and notifications now provide faster,
clearer feedback for keyboard and screen-reader users.

## Improved

- Wishlist discovery filters as people type, offers a named clear action, and
  announces when no wishlists match.
- Friend invitation email guidance is connected to its field, empty
  submissions are disabled, and feedback is announced.
- The notification button communicates whether its modal panel is open,
  keeps background controls unavailable, and returns focus to the button when
  it closes.
- Notification deletion names the selected notification, explains that the
  action is permanent, contains keyboard focus, and starts on the safe action.
- Notification actions expose busy states and announce successful or failed
  updates instead of failing silently.
