# OpenWish 0.1.16

OpenWish account settings now make identity, sign-in, and personal-data
choices easier to understand and safer to navigate on any screen.

## Improved

- Account settings navigation stays labelled, reachable, and comfortably
  sized on smaller screens.
- Profile settings explain read-only and optional fields, support phone
  autofill, and clearly name the save action.
- Email settings show confirmation in text and explain when a changed address
  takes effect.
- Password settings connect concise requirements to the new-password field.
- Personal-data actions name their outcomes and provide a clear safe exit
  before account deletion.
