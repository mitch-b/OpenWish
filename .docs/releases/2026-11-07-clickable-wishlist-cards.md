# Open wishlists from the whole card - November 7, 2026

Click or tap anywhere on a wishlist card to open its list, including its name,
icon, item count, and empty space. The overflow menu stays separate: opening
it or choosing Delete does not navigate away, and deletion still requires
confirmation.

Keyboard users can focus the card's named "Open wishlist" link and press Enter.
The focus outline now highlights the whole card. Normal link behavior, including
opening the wishlist in a new tab, is preserved.
