# Gift match refinement - September 24, 2026

Twenty focused UI/UX refinements make the post-draw gift match the clearest
decision point for a shopper, without changing who can see assignments:

1. Make the event heading quieter after the draw so the match leads.
2. Keep the event name and description readable without a second gradient.
3. Make the organizer's management link secondary to the shopping task.
4. Identify the match section with a proper heading.
5. Make the recipient's name the largest content in that section.
6. Replace green status styling with a restrained violet identity accent.
7. Move the suggested budget beside the recipient's name.
8. Include the exchange date beside the budget.
9. Say explicitly when no budget has been suggested.
10. Show how many gift ideas are available before opening the wishlist.
11. Link directly to the matched recipient's wishlist as the primary next step.
12. Explain that reserving an idea prevents duplicate gifts.
13. Keep the match and reservation privacy promise near that action.
14. Distinguish an attached wishlist with no ideas from one not shared yet.
15. Choose a recipient's wishlist with ideas over their empty attached lists.
16. Show an explicit state when no assignment is available after the draw.
17. Keep loading and retry feedback within the match section.
18. Give the mobile wishlist action a full-width, 44px touch target.
19. Allow long recipient names and action labels to wrap without overflow.
20. Arrange details deliberately at tablet and narrow-phone widths, with
    legible contrast in both themes.
