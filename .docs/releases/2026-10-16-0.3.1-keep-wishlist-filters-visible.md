# Keep your wishlist filters in view - October 16, 2026

Filtering a wishlist now keeps its context on screen, so people can see why
fewer ideas are showing and adjust one choice at a time.

- A visible summary reports the active result count whenever a search or
  filter narrows a wishlist.
- Remove a search, priority, availability, comment, price, or budget choice
  directly from that summary without reopening the filters.
- No-match guidance repeats the search phrase and offers one action to return
  to every idea.
- The filter-panel reset and the complete reset now use distinct labels.
- Item actions wrap cleanly on phones, keeping coordination and owner controls
  reachable without horizontal scrolling.

These controls remain local to the viewer's current wishlist session and do
not reveal reservations, comments, or private ideas to anyone else.
