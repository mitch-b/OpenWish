# Keep old wishlists without keeping them in the way

- Wishlist owners can move lists into **Wishlists → Archive**. While archived, friends, contributors, event participants, and connected apps can't access the list. Restoring it brings back its existing sharing permissions.
- Wishlist deletion previews the gift ideas you can see before you confirm. Event deletion shows visible ideas on linked wishlists and keeps those lists and ideas with their owners.
- Integrations follows the standard page layout, with Connected apps first and a separate MCP section for AI assistant connections and setup. The navigation uses the same gift mark as the favicon.
- **Cancel reservation** stays legible on hover in light and dark mode.
