# Reliable card actions and event setup - November 7, 2026

Wishlist and event overflow buttons show a clear hover, keyboard-focus, and
open-menu state. Click or tap anywhere else on an event card to open it or
continue setup, just like a wishlist card. Overflow menus remain independent
and are no longer clipped by event cards.

The organizer's Invite people, Add wishlist, Review lists, and Go to draw
shortcuts now stay on the current event and jump to the relevant section
instead of taking you home. Participant and shopper wishlist shortcuts stay
on their event too.
