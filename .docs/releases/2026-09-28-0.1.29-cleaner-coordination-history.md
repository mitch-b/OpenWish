# Cleaner coordination history - September 28, 2026

Five small reliability and privacy improvements keep coordination clearer:

1. Inviting someone who is already your friend by email reports a conflict rather than an unexpected error.
2. Batch invitations send only one email per address, regardless of capitalization, and continue with valid addresses even if one entry is invalid.
3. Removed notifications cannot be deleted a second time or marked as read.
4. Removed activity records stay out of your personal activity feed.
5. Friends' activity no longer shows entries for removed wishlists or gift ideas.

Focused service tests cover all five changes. The isolated browser journey also
checks existing-friend invitations, repeat notification deletion, authenticated
activity APIs, populated coordination screens, and desktop/mobile layouts.
