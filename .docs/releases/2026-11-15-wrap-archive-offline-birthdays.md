# Wrapping, archiving, offline, and birthdays - November 15, 2026

Five small improvements that make gift planning calmer.

- **Wrapped gifts.** On your gift plan, mark a bought gift as wrapped. The
  summary reads "N bought, M wrapped". Releasing or un-buying a gift clears it.
  Only you can see or change your wrapped status.
- **Archive instead of delete.** In "Still want these?" an old idea can be
  archived. Archived ideas disappear from shoppers' views, activity, event gift
  ideas and shared text, and stay under "Archived" on your list where you can
  restore them. Anyone who already reserved an archived idea keeps it on their
  gift plan.
- **Installable and offline-friendly.** OpenWish registers a small service
  worker. When you open it without a connection you see a friendly offline page
  instead of a browser error. Only that page is stored on your device; no
  wishlist, account, or API data is cached.
- **Birthdays in your calendar.** Download one `.ics` file of the friends'
  birthdays you already see on your dashboard. Each repeats yearly, with no
  birth year.
- **Birthday gift nudge.** When a friend's birthday is coming up and you have
  reserved gifts for them that you have not bought yet, the birthday card says
  how many remain and links to your gift plan.

Privacy is unchanged: reservations stay hidden from list owners, and the
calendar only contains friends who already share their birthday with you.
