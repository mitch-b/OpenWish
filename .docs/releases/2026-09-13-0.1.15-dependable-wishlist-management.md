# OpenWish 0.1.15

OpenWish now keeps wishlist management clear, keyboard-friendly, and
recoverable when loading or access updates are interrupted.

## Improved

- Wishlist cards provide explicit named links for keyboard and screen-reader
  navigation.
- Wishlist settings show meaningful progress and offer a retry when loading
  fails.
- Friend-access failures remain distinct from a genuinely empty access list.
- Sharing controls have clear names, prevent duplicate changes, announce
  outcomes, and provide comfortable touch targets.
- Event connections distinguish loading failures from having no available
  events and provide safe retry guidance.
