# A calmer first family exchange - November 7, 2026

An event invitation now carries your invited email through account creation and
sign-in. Confirmation emails and replacement links keep your place, and the
confirmation page gives you a clear way to sign in and continue. Broken
confirmation links and unavailable invitations offer recovery instead of an
error page. Email invitations recognize existing accounts regardless of case
or surrounding spaces.

Creating a wishlist inside an event now starts with "My gift ideas" and explains
the sharing that actually applies: event participants can see it, but your gift
giver's reservations stay hidden. You no longer have to decide between unrelated
friend-sharing settings or accidentally choose a private list that can't join
the event. A clear first-idea action follows creation.
New event lists are shared with event participants, not automatically with
friends outside the exchange.

An empty list isn't the same as being ready. Participants with no shared ideas
get a direct add-ideas action before and after the draw. Organizer readiness
waits for actual idea counts, excluding private, deleted, and received ideas.
Wishlist removal contains keyboard focus, restores it when dismissed, and
keeps failures visible so you can recover without losing your place. Event
cards also keep a visible focus outline across their controls.
Post-draw reminders stay neutral when surprise ideas are hidden from the owner.
Invalid confirmation links never prefill recovery forms with an account's email.

Guests still use ordinary accounts and email confirmation; this does not
introduce anonymous access or relax the exchange's privacy boundaries. Before
sending family invitations, the host should verify that outgoing confirmation
and invitation emails arrive successfully.
